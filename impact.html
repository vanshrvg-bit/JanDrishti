About

Why Jan Drishti

Jan Drishti was built for the "Code for the Nation" thematic competition to reimagine civic reporting as a shared, measurable neighbourhood health profile — not a one-way complaint box. It combines a civic dashboard, an interactive city map, and a community verification system so citizens can see the effect of collective action, not just file a ticket and wait.

Prototype note

About this data

All scores, issues, citizen counts and photos on this site are illustrative demo data generated for the prototype. Civic Priority Scores are a Jan Drishti prototype estimate — not an official government algorithm — used here to demonstrate how reported issues could be triaged.
